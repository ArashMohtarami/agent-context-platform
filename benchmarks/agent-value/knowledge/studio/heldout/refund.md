# Cancellation credit, current

Record: studio-refund
Revision: 2
Status: active
Source: fictional project operations handbook

Revision 2 credits the full paid cents when cancellation is at least 24 hours before the booking. Below 24 hours the credit is 75 percent, rounded down to whole cents. At or after start there is no credit. A cancellation creates one credit row and marks the booking cancelled. Repeating cancellation returns the same credit and does not issue money twice.
