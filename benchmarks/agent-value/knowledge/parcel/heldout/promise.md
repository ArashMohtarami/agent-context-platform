# Dispatch promise, current

Record: parcel-promise
Revision: 2
Status: active
Source: fictional project operations handbook

Current revision 2 uses a 16:00 UTC cutoff. An order exactly at the cutoff departs on the next business day. Friday after cutoff departs Monday; Saturday and Sunday also depart Monday. Before cutoff on a business day departs that date. Delivery is two business days after departure. Dates are ISO strings and input timestamps must carry an offset.
